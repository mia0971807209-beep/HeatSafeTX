Heat safety guide

Small actions. Big protection.

Simple precautions can help reduce the risk of heat-related illness.

💧

Drink water regularly

Stay hydrated throughout the day, especially during physical activity. Follow any fluid restrictions given by your clinician.

🌳

Take cooling breaks

Rest in shade or air-conditioned spaces and reduce strenuous activity during the hottest hours.

👕

Dress for the weather

Wear lightweight, loose-fitting clothing and use sun protection when outdoors.

🤝

Check on others

Older adults, young children, outdoor workers, and people with certain health conditions may need extra support.

🚨 Know the warning signs. Heat exhaustion may cause heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, or nausea. Move to a cooler place and seek medical advice if symptoms worsen or do not improve. Confusion, fainting, seizures, or very high body temperature may signal heatstroke: call 911 immediately. Begin cooling the person while waiting for help.
